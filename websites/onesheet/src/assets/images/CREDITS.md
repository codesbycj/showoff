[StockSnap](https://stocksnap.io), found through the
[Openverse](https://openverse.org) API, which is where the licence and the
photographer's name below come from. CC0 asks for nothing; they are credited
because the photographers did the work.

Each is centre-cropped to 4:5 and written at 900x1125 WebP, q82 — 24 to 56KB
apiece. That size is the card at 2x on a 1440 viewport, where it paints at
411x514, and nothing larger would ever be seen: the deck is the only place any
of them appears.

They are bundled rather than hotlinked. A measurement run that has to wait on
somebody else's CDN is a measurement of their CDN.

`tools/` has nothing to re-run here; the fetch-and-crop was a one-off and the
sources are linked above.

# Image credits

The eight card covers are **public domain** photographs from the US Farm Security
Administration / Office of War Information colour collection, 1940–1943 — the
Kodachrome transparencies held by the Library of Congress. They are United States
federal government works and carry no known copyright restrictions.

Each was retrieved from Wikimedia Commons, which reports the licence per file;
every one below came back as `Public domain`. Each was then centre-cropped to
16:10 and resized to 1024×640, and re-encoded as WebP. Nothing else was altered —
the tone lift the cards apply is done at runtime in `src/textures/cover.ts`, not
baked into these files.

They stand in for a fictional designer's project covers. The projects, the studio
and the person are invented; these photographs are not of them and have no
connection to them.

## Note on `ninth-house.webp`

That file is a restored version uploaded to Commons, and its `Artist` field holds
the filename rather than a name — so the attribution above is taken from the
photograph itself rather than from the file's metadata, and is the one entry here
not machine-verified. The licence *is* machine-verified as public domain, and the
file page is linked above so the chain can be checked. If the attribution matters
for your use, confirm it there rather than taking this table's word for it.

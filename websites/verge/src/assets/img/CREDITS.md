That matters more than usual here. The reference's customer band is a row of real
companies' logos; a rebuild cannot use those, and inventing fictional logos still means
drawing marks. So the `verge` customer band is **eight invented company names set in the
page's own display face**, which sidesteps the problem and reads better than eight
mismatched logos ever do.

verge is invented. The company, the three schemes, the prices, the statistics, the
customers and every line of copy are mine, and the footer says so on both designs.

why they are sourced separately from the after's.

## Fonts

| Font | Licence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Bricolage Grotesque Variable | SIL OFL 1.1, via `@fontsource-variable/bricolage-grotesque` | The display face. It has a real optical-size axis and slightly odd proportions, which is what lets a 92px headline look drawn rather than scaled. |
| Public Sans Variable | SIL OFL 1.1, via `@fontsource-variable/public-sans` | The text face: a plain, open grotesque designed for government forms, which is the right register for "what this costs and how it works". |

Neither is episode 1's Geist or episode 2's Inter Tight. The before uses no webfont at
all — Georgia, Trebuchet MS and Arial Black, which is what a bought theme actually ships
and part of why it looks the way it does.

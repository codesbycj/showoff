the board's palette, copy, hierarchy and block order at real 393dp sizes (I1,

that is a picture of something (I4), fill the foot with content rather than
padding (I3).

## Out of drawing range (I4) — the asset plan

Photographs, free-licensed Pexels, bundled locally, `CREDITS.md` per showcase,
every frame checked at 1:1 for other brands' marks.

- **Interiors and places** — `nordhome` `nordsearch` `nordstay` `alpenstay`
  `alpenpick` `fernhome` `fernlist` `ferntrip` `wisetrip` `wiseplaces`.
- **Food** — `verdestart` `verdehome` `verdedish` `flowmeals` `flowrecipe`.
  The board's category row is chips, not photo tiles, but the dish grid is
  photography and so is every meal card.
- **People** — `flowstart` `flowhome` `flowtrain` `lumofeed` `lumoreel`
  `lumoprofile` `kovahome` `kovastats` `orbithome` `orbitsend` `orbitchart`
  `ferntrip` `stridehome` `skyticket` `nordstay`.
- **Products** — `volthome` `voltitem` `voltcart`. See the brand note below:
  the board's products are real, so both the names and the photographs are
  substituted.
- **Maps** — `stridestart` (route over a road grid) and `wisefriends` (a dot
  field, not a world map). Built as the map *idea*, never traced; no real place
  is depicted and every street name is invented.
- **3D renders** — the fries mascot on food/03, the megaphone on
  social-media-app/02's featured card. Rebuilt as the *idea* in-app or dropped
  for a drawn mark; never traced.

**`flowstart` / `flowtrain` / `flowmeals` / `flowrecipe` replace illustration
with photography.** The health boards draw their people and their food as flat
illustration. An illustration of a person is still a picture *of* a person, and
reproducing that particular drawing is reproducing an arrangement — so the
subject is kept and the medium changes, which I4's last paragraph licenses.

## Reference mistakes to fix (I1) — found while reading the boards

- **crypto-wallet/02**: "Recieve" → "Receive".
- **social-media-app/02**: "Massage" → "Messages" on every dock (three
  screens); "Pro file" wraps mid-word in the dock; "@rayyan.offical" →
  "official"; a real band named in the audio line.
- **food/03**: "Reating" → "Rating"; "170 kalories" → "170 kcal"; "Minerale" →
  "Minerals"; the welcome body is two sentences of prose — R5 trims it.
- **ecommerce-app/02**: the product blurb is three lines of marketing copy
  ending in "Read more" — trimmed to a label row; `02:59:23` flash-sale timer
  counts, so it is wired to a real countdown rather than typed.
- **travel-app/02**: "Searching…" sits in a card whose result count already
  reads "14 Tickets found"; the two states cannot both be true, so the card
  resolves. Emoji flags → drawn flag discs (I8: flags are missing on Windows).
- **travel-app/03**: a waving-hand and a sun emoji as UI → drawn glyphs.
- **health/02**: the dock's active dot sits under *Home* while the phase card
  belongs to *Cycle*; the week strip starts on M with no date numbers.
- **fintech/02**: a gift emoji on the referral card → a drawn mark; a real
  design tool named in the transaction list.
- **booking-app/02**: the price bar reads "€384" for "3 nights" at "€91/night",
  which is 273 — R9 says the picture agrees with the numbers, so the total is
  computed from the nightly rate and the stay.
- Contrast and tap targets per screen against R4/R7 — several boards set muted
  grey under 4.5:1 and draw 32–36dp controls.

## Risks, stated up front

- **40 screens is larger than batch 4's 35 and larger than the 29 that were
  deleted.** This is a multi-session programme.
- **`flowstart`…`flowrecipe` is a five-screen app across two boards**, so its
  dock, type scale and palette have to agree across both or the set reads as
  two apps.
- **Eleven of thirteen apps are photography-led.** Step 5 (assets before
  components) is the long pole, not the layout.
- **Two maps and two illustration-replacement passes** are the most invention
  in the batch, and both are named above rather than discovered later.
- **The catalog is a boot-route file.** `showcase.js rm` rewrote
  `src/app/index.tsx` as a redirect; `node tools/showcase.js catalog` puts the
  wall back, and `python tools/catalog.py` reshoots it.

## Order of work

Per app, in table order: read the board → scaffold with the server stopped →
`theme` `layout` `use-insets` `text` `data` → assets → components one at a time
→ design bar (`audit.js` 0, `foot.py` ≤24dp) → **stop at I6 and show the
screens** → staging and the done-gate only after sign-off.

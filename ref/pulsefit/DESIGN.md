## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an energetic, uplifting, and frictionless approach to personal health, nutrition, and daily wellness tracking. Rooted in modern mobile-first wellness sensibilities, the interface balances clinical clarity with joyful encouragement. It strips away intimidation and numerical anxiety through generous whitespace, plush rounded card geometry, soft ambient illumination, and vibrant bio-positive lime accents.

The target demographic spans active lifestyle enthusiasts, mindful nutrition trackers, and individuals seeking structured daily habits without visual clutter. The visual character mixes contemporary iOS Human Interface elegance with crisp editorial hierarchy and tactile, approachable components. Micro-surfaces, pill-shaped chips, and pastel telemetry cues foster feelings of optimism, momentum, and daily accomplishment.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a high-energy bio-lime primary system, balanced against pristine slate neutrals and dedicated telemetry accents:

- **Primary Accent (Lime / Chartreuse):**
  - Primary Base: `#84CC16` (Interactive highlights, primary action triggers, active day markers, prominent central scanner FAB).
  - Primary Light: `#A3E635` (Gradients, progress gauge highlights, hover/touch responses).
  - Primary Deep: `#65A30D` (High-contrast typography on tinted backgrounds, micro-copy, data boundaries).
  - Primary Container / Glow: `#ECFCCB` and `#F7FEE7` (Featured hero cards, goal completion banners, active pill containers).

- **Neutral Foundations:**
  - Canvas / Screen Background: `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` (Subtle off-white slate foundation keeping contrast calm and glare-free).
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF` (Primary elevated cards, list items, navigation dock background).
  - Border & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` with soft alpha variants `#F1F5F9`.

- **Typography & Structural Slates:**
  - Slate Dark (Headlines, primary metric figures): `#0F172A`.
  - Slate Medium (Body text, labels, secondary units): `#475569`.
  - Slate Subtle (Inactive icons, captions, timestamps, ghost dates): `#94A3B8`.

- **Metric Telemetry Accents (Soft badges & health categories):**
  - Steps & Energy (Coral / Orange): Tint `#FFF7ED`, Accent `#F97316`.
  - Hydration (Cyan / Blue): Tint `#F0F9FF`, Accent `#0EA5E9`.
  - Heart Rate / BPM (Crimson / Rose): Tint `#FFF1F2`, Accent `#F43F5E`.
  - Exercise & Movement (Emerald): Tint `#ECFDF5`, Accent `#10B981`.
  - Weight & Mass (Amber): Tint `#FEFCE8`, Accent `#EAB308`.

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, friendly open apertures, and robust weight distribution to provide immediate scannability for numeric data.

- **Numerals & Metrics:** Large counter figures (`display-md` and `metric-numeral`) utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`, `800`) to anchor dashboard visual weight. Measurement units (e.g., "Kcal", "steps", "glass") always sit in lighter weights (`500` or `400`) at `13px` to `14px` alongside primary values.
- **Section Headers & Titles:** Set with punchy semi-bold or bold treatments, remaining compact to preserve vertical density on mobile screens.
- **Micro-labels & Status Badges:** Set in `label-sm` or `label-md` with subtle uppercase tracking to optimize readability within compact pill containers and metric tiles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is optimized for high-density, touch-friendly mobile performance:

- **Canvas Rhythm:** A standard mobile viewport utilizes a `16px` (`1rem`) to `20px` (`1.25rem`) side margin. Stacked modules observe an `8px` grid cadence, with inter-card spacing standardized at `12px` to `16px` to maintain visual grouping without crowding.
- **2-Column Tile Grid:** Telemetry cards (Steps, Water, Exercise, BPM) split into a symmetrical 2-column grid using a `12px` (`0.75rem`) gutter, providing balanced square or slightly portrait ratios for glanceable data reading.
- **Horizontal Scrollers:** Calendar day selectors and meal photo previews break outer margins via edge-to-edge overflow scrolling with standard interior padding.
- **Floating Dock Clearance:** A persistent bottom clearance of `96px` is enforced across all scrollable views to avoid occlusion by the elevated navigation dock and floating action button.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on clean surface layering, micro-borders, and soft ambient drop shadows rather than heavy elevation:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat base neutral `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Standard Cards & Tiles):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` with a faint structural border (`1px solid #F1F5F9`) and a soft diffused shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hero & Progress Containers):** Tonal green background `#ECFCCB` or subtle gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #ECFCCB 0%, #F7FEE7 100%)`) with a soft matched perimeter glow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Button & Navigation Bar):** The central quick-add button features high kinetic depth: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -2px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.45)`. The bottom navigation bar rests elevated at `box-shadow: 0 -4px 24px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` with subtle glassmorphic backdrop-filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-friendly, organic rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`):

- **Cards & Data Modules:** Radii range from `20px` to `24px` (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), producing a soft, pebble-like feel that eliminates harsh visual boundaries.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Action buttons, pill selectors, category tags, and calendar day chips feature fully rounded pill geometries (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Inner Containers & Metric Icon Badges:** Nested elements use `12px` to `16px` radii, harmonizing with outer card curvatures to maintain proportional concentric corner spacing.
- **Progress Elements:** Bar charts, segmented indicators, and circular tracker rings feature fully rounded stroke caps (`stroke-linecap: round`).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Lime Pill):** Background `#84CC16`, foreground text `#0F172A` or `#FFFFFF` (heavy font weight `700`), fully rounded pill shape. Pressed state scales down slightly (`scale(0.98)`) with background darkening to `#65A30D`.
- **Icon Action Buttons (Small Add `+`):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` or tinted neutral circle (`32px` to `36px`), containing dark slate or lime iconography, with subtle ambient shadow.
- **Central Action Button (FAB):** High-prominence 56px circle or rounded squircle centered in the navigation bar. Lime green `#84CC16` with icon glyph in `#0F172A`, accented by radial lime glow shadow.

### Cards & Telemetry Tiles
- **Hero Weekly Progress Card:** Tonal light-lime container (`#ECFCCB`), housing progress summary, bold typography, and an integrated circular SVG progress ring with inner metric badges (e.g., "6 days").
- **Metric Micro-Cards:** White background with two vertical zones: top row contains metric name and tinted circular badge (e.g., soft orange with flame icon); bottom row displays large bold counter and unit label.
- **Horizontal Calendar Strip:** Segmented row displaying day abbreviations (S, M, T, W, T, F, S) above numeric dates. The active current day is highlighted inside a full lime pill container (`#A3E635` / `#84CC16`) with dark slate text.

### Charts & Progress Rings
- **Segmented Bar Chart:** Vertical pill-capped bars. Inactive or previous days rendered in soft muted neutral `#F1F5F9` or striped/hatched textures; active or peak day highlighted in vibrant `#84CC16`. Value labels float above the bar in `label-sm`.
- **Circular Progress Gauge:** Dual-layer SVG ring. Track rendered in `#E2E8F0` or `#DCFCE7`, with animated progress stroke in `#84CC16`.

### Lists & Meal Logs
- **Meal Row Card:** Full-width white card housing meal category ("Breakfast"), calorie badge with pastel flame indicator, circular food thumbnail stacks, and circular quick-add icon button.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Dock Bar:** Elevated floating or pinned dock with translucent white surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), containing 5 item slots. Items use minimalist line icons (`24px`) with `11px` caption labels. The center slot is reserved for the prominent lime quick-action/scan button.

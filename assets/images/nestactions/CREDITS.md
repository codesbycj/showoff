# nestactions — image credits

Free-licensed portraits from **Pexels**, used under the Pexels licence, baked
by `tools/bake/faces.py nestactions`.

Every face on these screens is a photograph. A wallet's contact grid and its
ledger are lists of people, and initials on coloured discs would say the
opposite of what the screens are for (AGENTS.md, "Faces are photographs").

`face-1.jpg` is the account portrait in the header; the rest are the people
the wallet knows.

Everything else on these screens is drawn: the waving hand (the reference
sets it as an emoji), the bell, the four action discs, the coin marks, the
padlocks, the chart and the news marks.

**The coin marks are shapes, not logos.** Real coin marks belong to real
projects and are not copied; each of these is a letterform or a simple device
drawn for this showcase, on a tinted disc so the rows can be told apart.

The account name is gender-neutral. The portraits come from a fixed pool, so
a gendered name pinned to whichever face lands first says something about a
real person that the data has no business saying.

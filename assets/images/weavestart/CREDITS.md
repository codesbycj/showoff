# weavestart imagery

**None.** This showcase ships no image files.

The two marks on the sign-in buttons are drawn in `react-native-svg`: both are
trademarks and not redistributable, so these stand in for them — the
proportions are the design's, the shapes are this project's. They are small,
flat and geometric, which is squarely in drawing range.

Everything else on the screen is type on coloured pills.

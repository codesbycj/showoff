## There is no map here

A map is not in drawing range: it has no idea to interpret, so vectoring one
is tracing a specific arrangement rather than expressing an idea — copying
with extra steps (AGENTS.md, "A map is not in drawing range either").

No free-licensed static tile is bundled either. What is drawn is the map
*idea*: a flat ground, a faint rule for texture, a radius ring around where
the parcel started, a dashed route, and a pin at each end. None of the
reference's real street or district names are reused, and the two places are
invented.

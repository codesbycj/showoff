The streak card is a photograph because its job is to look appetising rather
than to report a number — it is the one block here that is a picture *of*
something.

**The type sits on a gradient, not on the frame.** A dark photograph is dark
*somewhere*: white text over the pale broth in this one is unreadable without
it, and a flat scrim across the whole card would dull the picture it is
there to show.

Everything on screen apart from the two photographs is drawn —
the rail, the knob, the bubble, the flame, the dumbbell and the dock glyphs.

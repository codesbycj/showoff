**The map is not a photograph and not a trace.** I4 puts a map out of drawing
range, and gives two honest routes; this build takes the second — the map
*idea* over a flat ground (land tone, park, river, road grid, radius ring), in
`src/features/tamarfind/components/map-ground.tsx`. No real place is depicted.

# finansaccount imagery

The portraits are free-licensed photographs from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)
under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use,
modification allowed, attribution not required. Credited here anyway, because
the photographers did the work. They are bundled rather than hotlinked, because
a recording must not depend on the network.

Baked to 240px square by `tools/bake/faces.py`, which crops each one on the
head — a centred crop of a portrait takes the collar. Pexels photo ids:

* `face-1.jpg` — pexels 12311572

Everything else on this screen is drawn: the glyphs are Lucide, which is paths
through `react-native-svg`, and the flags are drawn rather than set as emoji — Windows ships
no flag glyphs at all — and the scheme mark is sheared type.

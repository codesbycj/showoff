# focalplan — image credits

**No bundled imagery.** Every mark is drawn: the calendar glyph, the three
service marks, the hatched discs on the pencilled days and the legend's dots.

The reference names and wears two real products' logos in the "synced with"
row. A mark belongs to its owner, so the services here are invented and their
marks say what they do rather than who they are — see the note at the top of
`src/features/focalplan/data/start.ts`.

## Why these are photographs

A person's face is a picture, not an icon (AGENTS.md, "Faces are
photographs"). Initials on a coloured disc is the fallback state a real app
falls back *to*, not the design — a screen built from it is visibly missing
something the reference had, and the reference here shows four faces.

Each is square-cropped on the head and resampled once to 240px, which covers
every disc on this screen at 3x: a 46dp avatar in the header and three 34dp
discs on the card.

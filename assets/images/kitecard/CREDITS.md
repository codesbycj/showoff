# kitecard — image credits

**No bundled imagery.** Every mark on this screen is drawn: the issuer's
petal mark and kite on the card, the eye on "Show card details", the three
merchant marks on the charge list, and the four tab glyphs.

The reference puts a card network's mark on the card and two real companies'
logos in the rows beneath it. Neither is a showcase's to wear, so both are
replaced — see the note at the top of
`src/features/kitecard/data/start.ts`.

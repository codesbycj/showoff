Courts and the people on them are pictures *of* something, so they are
photographs; the chips, the date tile, the quick-action glyphs, the bat and
the dock are drawn (AGENTS.md, "Draw the interface, photograph the picture").

Frames rejected on sight: a rack of helmets carrying legible brand marks, and
a hall with a painted mural. Frames of children were left out of an adult
tournament app.

**The brand is invented** and set in this showcase's own typeface; the
reference's own wordmark is not copied.

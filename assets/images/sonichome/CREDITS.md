Baked by `tools/bake/sonic.py` from `tools/bake/sources/sonic/`.

**None of the board's own artwork is reproduced.** It uses real album covers
(The Weeknd's *Starboy*, an Eminem sleeve), those artists' names and the
SoundCloud wordmark. Every sleeve here is free-licensed photography standing in
for a fictional release, and every artist and section name is invented.

Cover art is a **photograph**, not a drawing (I4): a sleeve is a picture *of*
something.

**Checked at 1:1 for other brands' marks.** Pexels 16235687 was rejected for a
RODE wordmark across the microphone body — legible at full size, invisible on
a contact sheet.

# verdant imagery

**None.** This showcase ships no image files.

The reference design shot drops a 3D render of a leather wallet into the screen.
A render cannot be recreated and must not be traced badly, and the usual
stand-in — free-licensed stock — would have meant a brown leather wallet on a
white cyclorama, cut out and tinted green.

So the hero is rebuilt as the thing the render is a picture of: a pocket, two
cards, a lit disc and a pool of shadow, all drawn in `react-native-svg` in
`src/features/verdant/components/`. It is sharp at any size, it recolours with
the theme, it adds nothing to the bundle, and it can be revealed a piece at a
time on camera — which a dropped PNG cannot.

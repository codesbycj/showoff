# orbitstart — image credits

**No bundled imagery.** Everything on this screen is drawn: the holographic
sweep behind the card is a real `react-native-svg` gradient, the card is a
card component, and the two sign-in glyphs are paths.

The reference's 3D-rendered holographic board could not be recreated and must
not be traced badly (AGENTS.md), so it is rebuilt as the idea of itself — a
thick open ring on a gradient, cropped by the screen, with a real card posed
and shadowed on it.

On a dating app the faces are not decoration: the screen's whole claim is
that there are real people behind it, and initials on a coloured disc would
say the opposite (AGENTS.md, "Faces are photographs").

The rings, the arrow and the `®` are drawn — or, in the mark's case, simply
set: it is a character the typeface already has.

**The brand is invented.** The reference's own wordmark is not copied.

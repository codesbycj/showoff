**The reference draws its article art as flat illustrations of people. These
are photographs instead.** A drawn person is a picture *of* someone, which is
the bitmap side of the split (AGENTS.md, "Draw the interface, photograph the
picture") — and hand-vectoring a figure is the same mistake as hand-vectoring
a map: reproducing an arrangement rather than expressing an idea. The plan
screen of the same reference already uses photographs for exactly this.

`art-3` is cropped left of the water bottle in its frame. The bottle's
printed wrap is not identifiable, and a frame nobody can clear is not one to
rely on.

Everything else here is drawn: the search lens, the bell and its badge, the
plus, and the eye, reply and heart glyphs along each card's foot.

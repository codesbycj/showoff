# nibbleboards — image credits

Three free-licensed portraits from **Pexels**, used under the Pexels licence,
baked by `tools/bake/faces.py nibbleboards 3`.

| File | Pexels photo | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `face-1.jpg` | <https://www.pexels.com/photo/12311572/> | The account portrait in the greeting |

A person's face is a picture, not an icon (AGENTS.md, "Faces are
photographs"), and a share row built from initials on coloured discs is
visibly missing what the reference had.

Everything else on this screen is drawn: the burn ring, the water columns,
the five capsule gauges and their knobs, the plus glyphs and the three dock
glyphs.

Every name here is gender-neutral. The portraits come from a fixed pool, so a
gendered name pinned to whichever face lands first says something about a real
person that the data has no business saying.

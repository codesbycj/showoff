# voteng imagery

VoteNG is a fictional app. Nothing here is a record of a real election: the
"Election 2026" ballot, the percentages, the vote counts and the state
leaderboard are invented numbers written for a UI showcase. Nigeria's real
presidential election is not held in 2026.

## Portraits and party emblems — generated, not photographs

`obi.jpg`, `tinubu.jpg`, `atiku.jpg`, `kwankwaso.jpg` and the four
`party-*.jpg` files are AI-generated images that shipped inside the Stitch
export this showcase rebuilds. They are **likenesses, not photographs** of the
politicians they are labelled with, and the party marks are approximations
rather than the parties' registered emblems — the Labour Party badge even
carries garbled lettering, which is visible if you open the file at full size.

They are used here only because the screens have to show *something* in a 44dp
circle, and because the rebuild is measured against the export that contains
them. They are cropped and downscaled from 1024x1024, nothing else.

way the hero is. No real outlet published any of it.

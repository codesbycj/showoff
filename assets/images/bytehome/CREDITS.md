# bytehome imagery

The reference's Quick Send avatars are Memoji — a 3D render that cannot be
reproduced and must not be traced. These are free-licensed portraits from
Pexels, baked by `tools/bake/byte.py`.

The Apple, Amazon and PayPal marks on the ledger are drawn in
`src/features/bytehome/components/brand.tsx`, not bitmaps: a logo is vector
art in the real app and a 24dp raster of one is mush.

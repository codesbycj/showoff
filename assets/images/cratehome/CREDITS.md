## Why these are photographs, and why they are parcels

A parcel is a picture of something, so it is a bitmap (AGENTS.md, "Photograph
the picture"). The interface around it — the glyphs, the progress rails, the
tab bar — is drawn.

**The reference's rows are a Rolex and a Mac mini.** Both are real products,
and the obvious free-licensed substitutes are worse: the best earbud shots are
recognisably one manufacturer's design. A parcel app showing parcels avoids
the question and is the more honest subject anyway.

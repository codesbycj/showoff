## The split this screen is built on

**The road is photographed and the route is drawn.** A road is a picture of a
place and out of drawing range; a route line is a piece of interface. That is
also why there is no map here — a map has no idea to interpret, so vectoring
one is tracing the reference path by path rather than building anything
(AGENTS.md, "Know what is in drawing range").

The route is drawn in the measured box's own coordinates rather than in a
0–100 viewBox: a square viewBox stretched to a 393×477 box scales x and y by
different amounts and takes the stroke width with it, which turned a 9-unit
line into a 35dp blob.

## The standing statement

The brand, the address and every figure on the screen are invented. This is
not a photograph of that road.

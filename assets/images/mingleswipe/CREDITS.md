# mingleswipe — image credits

Two free-licensed photographs from **Pexels**, used under the Pexels licence,
cropped by `tools/bake/mingle.py`.

| File | Pexels photo | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | <https://www.pexels.com/photo/35555305/> | The full-bleed profile portrait |
| `face-1.jpg` | <https://www.pexels.com/photo/12311572/> | The account portrait on the top bar |

The hero is cropped to the phone's own aspect so it is resampled once rather
than squeezed from a 3:2 frame into a 1:2.17 screen.

Frames rejected on sight: a beanie carrying a legible wordmark, and every
frame of a child — neither belongs on a dating profile.

Everything laid over the photograph is drawn: the scrim gradients, the
location pin, the music, play and bolt glyphs on the interest chips, the
cross and the heart.

**The name does not imply a gender**, and nor does the bio. The portrait
comes from a fixed pool, so a gendered name pinned to whichever face lands
first says something about a real person that the data has no business
saying.

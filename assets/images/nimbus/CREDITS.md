# nimbus imagery

None. This showcase bundles no images at all.

Everything on the screen is drawn: the card's face is two SVG gradients, the
four action glyphs and the four dock glyphs are SVG paths, and the merchant
marks in the transaction list are drawn shapes and type rather than logo files
(Apple Music's rounded square and quavers, Amazon's smile, Netflix's N, and the
Uber Eats wordmark set in Inter).

The brand colours are the brands' own — Mastercard #eb001b / #f79e1b / #ff5f00,
Apple Music #fa233b, Amazon #1a6eb4, Netflix #e50914 — used at 15–33dp as the
marks they are, which is what makes each row readable at a glance.

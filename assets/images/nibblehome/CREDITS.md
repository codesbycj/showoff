A person's face is a picture, not an icon (AGENTS.md, "Faces are
photographs"). Everything else on this screen is drawn: the progress ring and
its knob, the search and bell glyphs, the unread dot, the bolt, the cup, the
sun, the sparkline, the macro rails and the three dock glyphs.

The account name is gender-neutral on purpose. The portrait comes from a fixed
pool, so pinning a gendered name to whichever face lands first says something
about a real person that the data has no business saying — which is what the
reference does.

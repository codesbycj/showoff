# zoneroute — image credits

**No photographs.** Everything on this screen is drawn.

**The map is not a photograph and not a trace.** I4 puts a map out of drawing
range and gives two honest routes; this build takes the second — the map *idea*
over a flat ground (land tone, park, road grid, route polyline, the two ends
and the rider on the line), in
`src/features/zoneroute/components/route-map.tsx`. No real place is depicted
and none is implied; the street names are invented.

**The brand is fictional.** The board is a McDonald's design throughout; none
of that is reproduced.

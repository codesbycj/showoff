Food is a picture *of* something, so the meal thumbnails are photographs
(AGENTS.md, "Draw the interface, photograph the picture"). A drawn bowl of
oats is a cartoon of a bowl of oats, and a column of them is what makes a
screen read as a toy.

Everything else on this screen is drawn: the calorie arc, the fork and knife,
the flame, the sparkle on the button, the checkboxes and the five dock glyphs.
The reference uses emoji for several of those, and an emoji is rendered by
whatever font the platform happens to have.

**One bowl in `4828100` is not used.** The top-left cup in that frame carries
embossed lettering on its inside wall, which a crop at thumbnail size would
show. The two bowls used were checked at 1:1 first.

Each food name is the food in its own photograph. The pool is fixed, so a row
cannot end up labelled as something it is not.

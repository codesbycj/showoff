# flowhome imagery

Brand photography is not redistributable; these stand in for it.
All frames are free-licensed Pexels stock, baked by `tools/bake/flow.py`.

**The board draws its people and its food as flat illustration.** An illustration
of a woman is still a picture *of* a woman, so the subject is kept and the medium
changes (I4).

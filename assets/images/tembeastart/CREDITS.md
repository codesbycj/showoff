Each is centre-cropped square and written at 120px, JPEG q86 — 36KB for all
seven. Nothing is recoloured, retouched or re-cropped beyond the square.
`tools/bake/tembea.py` does it.

**They are photographs on purpose.** A place is a picture of somewhere, and that
is out of drawing range however small it ends up on screen — a tag's thumbnail
is 30dp and it is still a photograph, not a drawn mark.

**Two candidates were rejected for carrying somebody else's mark**: Pexels
5574045 and 36298878 are the same safari vehicle, with a tour operator's
wordmark on the spare-wheel covers and a legible number plate. Neither is
visible at contact-sheet size; one of them was baked into this folder before
anyone looked at it at 1:1.

**The places are invented.** Mirama, Rugezi, Nyandu, Kivu Ridge, Akagera,
Mukura and Gishwati are not listings, and the photographs are not of them — the
tags are a design showing what the app is for. The brand, Tembea, is invented
too.

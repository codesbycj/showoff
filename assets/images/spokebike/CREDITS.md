## Why these are photographs

A bicycle is a large specific subject and is out of drawing range — vectoring
one produces a cartoon of a bike where the design had a bike (AGENTS.md, "Know
what is in drawing range"). Clean product shots of bikes on a studio sweep do
not exist in free stock, so these are crops of real bikes in real places: the
photograph fills the card's right half and is clipped by the card's own corner,
which is a crop rather than a cut-out.

The brand, the bike names and every figure on the screen are invented. These

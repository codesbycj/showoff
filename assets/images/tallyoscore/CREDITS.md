# tallyoscore — image credits

Two free-licensed portraits from **Pexels**, used under the Pexels licence,
baked by `tools/bake/faces.py tallyoscore 2`.

| File | Pexels photo | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `face-1.jpg` | <https://www.pexels.com/photo/12311572/> | Set inline in the headline sentence |
| `face-2.jpg` | <https://www.pexels.com/photo/30468665/> | The friend on the share bar |

A person's face is a picture, not an icon (AGENTS.md, "Faces are
photographs") — and the inline portrait is the whole conceit of the headline,
so initials on a disc would lose the screen's one idea.

**Everything the reference sets as an emoji is drawn here**: the waving hand,
the growth arrow, the trophy, the smiling face. An emoji is rendered by
whatever font the platform happens to have — different on Windows, Android
and iOS, and for some codepoints missing outright. Drawn, they take the
screen's own ink colour and look the same everywhere.

The brand mark is the four-diamond glyph in `components/glyphs.tsx`, drawn
for this showcase; the reference's own mark is not copied.

The name is gender-neutral. The portraits come from a fixed pool, so a
gendered name pinned to whichever face lands first says something about a real
person that the data has no business saying.

**The category tiles are photographs, not drawn icons** (I4). A row of round
tiles reading Burger / Steak / Sea Food is six pictures *of* food; drawing them
in SVG produces a row of cartoons where the design had food.

Baked by `tools/bake/zone.py` from `tools/bake/sources/zone/`.

**The brand is fictional.** The board is a McDonald's design throughout — its
mark, "Big Mac Burger", "McDonald's Maison" — and names DoorDash in its legal
line. None of that is reproduced: this is "Food Zone", with invented
restaurants and drawn marks of its own.

**Checked at 1:1 for other brands' marks.** Pexels 27988502 — the hero burger —
has a Coca-Cola can at the left of the frame, so every crop taken from it
starts at 26% of the width. A contact sheet cannot show a 40px mark; each frame
was opened at full size first.

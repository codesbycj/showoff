## Why these are photographs — and why the products are not

A customer is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference has a
photograph on its account row and this build has one on every customer.

The **products** go the other way. A shirt, a cap or a bag at 32px in an order row is a
*marker* for a line item, which is interface, and every real store admin draws it that
way. Photographing them would also mean finding nine unbranded product shots that read at
32px, which is the point at which rule 8's third option — keep it as interface — is the
honest one rather than the lazy one.

Everything else is drawn: the Dravelo leaves, all eight sidebar glyphs, the nine product
marks, the performance ring, the revenue columns and the report bars.

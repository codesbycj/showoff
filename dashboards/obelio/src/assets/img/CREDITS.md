| File | Pexels photo | Where it appears |

Free-licensed, used under the Pexels licence, standing in for the reference's own
photography, which is not redistributable. Square-cropped on the head and resampled once
to 240px, which covers a 36px disc at 3x and beyond. Baked for an earlier showcase in this
repository by `tools/bake/faces.py` and copied here rather than re-downloaded, so the
build has no network dependency.

The other five portraits this repository holds are not bundled: this design shows exactly
one person, and shipping assets nothing references is the "nothing unused shipped" half of
rule 8.

## Why it is a photograph

A person's face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference has a photograph here
and a build with a monogram in its place is visibly missing something it had.

Everything else is drawn: the Talero obelisk, all nine sidebar glyphs, the six vendor
marks, the four currency discs, the trophy and the crown on the upgrade panel, the
earnings bars and the category curve.

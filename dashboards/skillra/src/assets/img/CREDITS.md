Six, because the faculty is five and the learner is one. Each gets their own portrait: the
same face twice in one list reads as the same person twice.

## Why these are photographs, and why the course images are not

An instructor is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference
puts photographs on its own course cards too — but of _scenes_: a person at a desk, a
person at a laptop, a person in front of a blackboard.

A photograph of a scene is out of drawing range, and free-licensed unbranded stock of one
is not to hand. So the **subject** changed rather than the medium: a course card leads
with the instructor's portrait on a tinted ground, which is a photograph this build has
and the thing a learner picks a course by. The track tiles beside them are drawn glyphs,
because a track is an idea rather than a picture.

Everything else is drawn: the Skillra mark, all thirty-two interface glyphs, the player's
waveform, every meter and every level chip.

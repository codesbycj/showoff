reference's own photography, which is not redistributable. Square-cropped on the head and
resampled once to 240px, which covers the 44px driver portrait at 3x and beyond.

Six portraits across twelve drivers, two drivers to a face. The reference's own list
repeats faces too — a depot of twelve and a stock library of six is the honest trade, and
each pairing keeps the two drivers in different groups so the same face is never adjacent
to itself.

## Why these are photographs — and why there is no photograph of a van

A driver is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8).

A **van** is also a picture of a thing, which is why there is none here. The reference
puts a photograph of a Volkswagen Transporter in the vehicle card; unbranded photography
of a van barely exists, because every van carries a marque on its nose, and a drawn van
would be exactly the vector cartoon of a photographic subject rule 8 forbids. So the
vehicle card leads with the **load space**, drawn to scale with its length and width on
it: interface, not a picture.

Everything else is drawn: the Routra mark, all twenty-nine interface glyphs, the stacked
day bar, the weekly bars and the route timelines.

## Why these are photographs

Two places on this build show people: the activity manager, which is six colleagues doing
six things, and the promo card at the foot of the sidebar, which in the reference is a
photograph of a person pointing at the offer.

A face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). Initials on a coloured disc is a fallback
state, and a *drawn* figure in the promo card would be the exact failure the rule names —
a vector cartoon of a photographic subject at display size.

Six people, six portraits, no repeats.

Everything else is drawn: the Atelyra mark, every sidebar glyph, the waving hand, the
card's invented scheme mark, both dot matrices, the growth bars and the stock curve.

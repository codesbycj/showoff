## Why these are photographs

These rows are people — a freelancer's clients and the people they are being suggested to
connect with. A person's face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). Initials on a coloured
disc is a fallback state, not a substitute for the design: the reference has photographs
in every one of these rows, and a build with monograms in them is visibly missing
something it had.

Each row gets its own portrait. Reusing one across two rows reads as the same person twice.

Everything else on these panes is drawn: the TWISTY mark, every icon, the income chart,
the proposal strips, the premium card's halftone sphere and the wallet donut.

## Why these are photographs, and why the recordings are not

The team is people, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference puts
photographs in its Members card and beside every notification too.

**The reference's video tiles could not be kept, in either direction.** They are frames of
surgical footage, captioned with the National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery and a
Leica M530 OH6 — a real institution and a real instrument over invented figures, which is
the one thing this series must not produce. And a frame of footage is a photograph of a
thing, so it could not be drawn instead: unbranded stock of an operating microscope does
not exist, and tracing one would be copying rather than expressing an idea.

So the **subject** changed rather than the medium. What a workspace's recording list needs
is how long a session is and where the talking happens, and that is interface: every
recording carries a drawn waveform, its length, its chapters and who was in it. The two 3D
renders in the hero and the upsell went the same way — a render cannot be recreated and
must not be traced, and neither of them was carrying any information.

Everything else is drawn: the Hivora mark, all twenty-eight interface glyphs, every
waveform, the stacked day chart, the week strip and every meter.

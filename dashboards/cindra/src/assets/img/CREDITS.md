## Why these are photographs

Recent Activity is six people doing six things. A face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8),
and the reference has a photograph on every one of those rows — initials on a coloured
disc is a fallback state, not a substitute.

Each row gets its own portrait: six rows, six portraits, no repeats.

Everything else is drawn: the Corvane coins, all ten sidebar glyphs, the four quick
actions, the four plan glyphs, the five footer marks, the balance card with its
contactless arcs, the cashflow columns and the statistic ring.

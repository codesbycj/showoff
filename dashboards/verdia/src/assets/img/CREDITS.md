# verdia — image credits

One bundled photograph: the account portrait in the header.

| File | Pexels photo | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `face-1.jpg` | <https://www.pexels.com/photo/12311572/> | The account portrait beside "Hello, Marisol" |

Free-licensed, used under the Pexels licence, standing in for the reference's own
photography, which is not redistributable. Square-cropped on the head and resampled once
to 240px, which covers a 42px disc at 3x. Baked for an earlier showcase in this repository
by `tools/bake/faces.py` and copied here rather than re-downloaded, so the build has no
network dependency.

## Why it is a photograph

A person's face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). Initials on a coloured disc is a
fallback state, not a substitute: the reference has a photograph here and a build with a
monogram in its place is visibly missing something it had.

Everything else on these three tabs is drawn: the Pennora leaf, every icon, the six
counterparty marks, the three currency discs, the payment card and its chip, the spending
ring, the allocation ring, the net-worth curve and the week's bars.

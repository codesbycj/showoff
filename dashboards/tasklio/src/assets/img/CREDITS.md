Six, because the studio is six. Each row gets its own portrait: the same face twice in one
list reads as the same person twice.

## Why these are photographs, and why nothing else is

The studio is people, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference puts
photographs on its cards and above its board too.

Everything else on this build is drawn, because everything else on it is interface: the
Tasklio mark, all twenty-two glyphs, every meter, the column dots, the tag chips and the
client bars. The reference's cards carry small screenshots of other designs inside two of
them — a picture of somebody else's interface, which is neither ours to show nor anything
a task card needs.

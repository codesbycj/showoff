reference's own photography, which is not redistributable. Square-cropped on the head and
resampled once to 240px, which covers the 40px account disc at 3x and beyond.

Baked for an earlier showcase in this repository by `tools/bake/faces.py` and copied here
rather than re-downloaded, so the build has no network dependency.

Five, because the firm has five fee earners and every one of them appears: in the Progress
Case list, in the workload card on Task, against a matter in the list, and three of them
in the messages panel. Nothing unused is shipped (rule 8).

The names are invented. The faces are real people who had nothing to do with a law firm
that does not exist.

## Why these are photographs

A fee earner is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference
puts a photograph on the account and another beside the case assignee; initials on a
coloured disc is a fallback state, not a substitute.

Everything else is drawn: the Caldrow mark, all twenty-six interface glyphs, the profit
curve, the billable-hours cells with their hatch, the billed-and-collected bars, the
meters and the priority pills.

## Why that one is a photograph, and why the token discs are not

The account is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference puts
a photograph there too.

The asset discs are **drawn** — a pastel circle carrying the ticker's first two letters.
A token's logo is a mark belonging to a project, and this build invents every figure
beside it; carrying a real mark over invented balances is the one thing this series must
not produce. The pastel is the same token the holding uses everywhere else, so a disc and
its row cannot drift apart.

Everything else is drawn: the Lunara mark (three waxing discs), all twenty-seven interface
glyphs, the portfolio curve, the seven-day sparklines, the allocation tiles and the buy-
cohort grid.

## Why these are photographs, and why the truck is not

A driver is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference puts a
photograph on its driver card too. Initials on a coloured disc is a fallback state, not a
substitute.

The truck is **not** a photograph, and not a drawing of a truck either. The reference puts
a small isometric truck glyph on its vehicle card, which is interface — a mark, not a
picture of a particular lorry — so it is drawn. Had the design led on a photograph of the
vehicle, free-licensed unbranded ones barely exist, because every truck carries a marque
on its nose.

The map is gone for the same reason from the other direction: a map is a picture of a
place, so drawing one is tracing rather than expressing an idea. See the README's
declared departures.

Everything else is drawn: the Haulvo mark, all rail and interface glyphs, the leg chips,
the utilisation meters, the stop timelines, the bar chart and the leg bars.

Baked for an earlier showcase in this repository by `tools/bake/faces.py` and copied here
rather than re-downloaded, so the build has no network dependency.

Five, because the team has five people and every one of them appears: on a quota ring, on
a deal card, as a lead owner. The names are invented; the faces are real people who have
nothing to do with a CRM that does not exist.

## Why these are photographs, and why the flags are not

A rep is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference puts
photographs on its team list too.

The country discs are **drawn** — two or three bands in a circle. A flag is interface at
that size, and an emoji flag is not an option: Windows has no flag glyphs at all, so one
ships as two letters in a box. They are also not traced: at 24px the bands are the whole
signal and a crest would be mud.

Everything else is drawn: the Prospra mark, all twenty-eight interface glyphs, the
three-series revenue chart, the quota rings, the platform bars and the pipeline columns.

Four, because the activity feed shows four senders and the header one account. Shipping
the others this repository holds would be assets nothing references, which is the "nothing
unused shipped" half of rule 8.

## Why these are photographs, and why the company discs are not

A sender is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The reference puts a
photograph on its account too.

The company discs are **drawn** — an initial on a tinted square. A portfolio company's
logo is its own mark, and every figure beside it here is invented: a real mark over an
invented valuation is the one thing this series must not produce.

Everything else is drawn: the vantyr mark, all thirty-two interface glyphs, the step
chart, the syndicate meters and the status dots.

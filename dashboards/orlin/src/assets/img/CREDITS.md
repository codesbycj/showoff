## Why these are photographs

The transactions history is six people, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8).
The reference has a photograph on every one of those rows, and initials on a coloured disc
is a fallback state rather than a substitute.

Six counterparties, six portraits, no repeats.

Everything else is drawn: the Stakvio dots, every nav and rail glyph, the summary bars, the
three activity traces and the wallet curve.

## Why these are photographs

The mandatory-payments stack is people who are owed money, and a face is a picture, not an
icon (rule 8). The reference has photographs there and on its account row; initials on a
coloured disc is a fallback state, not a substitute.

Four are shown and two sit behind the "+2", which is the reference's own arrangement —
and each of the four is a different person, because the same face twice in one stack reads
as the same person twice.

Everything else is drawn: the Quilbo ring, every rail and header glyph, the six
counterparty marks, the payment card with its contactless arcs, the hatched engagement
bars and the balance curve.

Only one, because this design shows exactly one person — the account in the top right.
Shipping the other portraits this repository holds would be assets nothing references,
which is the "nothing unused shipped" half of rule 8.

## Why that one is a photograph

The account disc is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The
reference puts a photograph there too.

Everything else is drawn: the Metriqo mark, all eight sidebar glyphs, the sun and moon on
the appearance switch, the four KPI glyphs, the three device glyphs, the source glyphs,
the time-of-day heatmap, the active-users curve, the device ring and the conversion
funnel. The sun and the moon are the ones that matter most here — they are the glyphs
that differ most across platforms, and an emoji pair would have rendered three different
ways on three machines.

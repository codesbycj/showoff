## Why that one is a photograph, and why the asset discs are not

The account holder is a person, and a face is a picture, not an icon (rule 8). The
reference puts a photograph there too.

The asset discs are **drawn** — a letter on the asset's own tint. A token's logo is a mark
belonging to that project, and every figure beside it on this build is invented; carrying
a real mark over invented balances is the one thing this series must not produce. The
tints are chosen so a white letter clears 4.5:1 on each of them, which is also what makes
the leader bars and the candle bodies readable on white.

Everything else is drawn: the Zyntrex mark, all thirty interface glyphs, the portfolio
value chart, the candlesticks and their volume band, the order book's depth shading, the
leader bars and the market table's sparklines.
